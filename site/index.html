Questions

Good to know

Where is TAKE in Canggu?

In Perum Prapta Canggu Asri, Blok D No.1, on Jl. Krisnantara 1, a quiet lane off Jalan Pantai Berawa in Tibubeneng. TAKE Coffee and TAKE Scooter Rental share the white house with the two signs.

What are the opening hours?

Every day from 7am to 8pm, for the kitchen and the rental desk.

Do you deliver food?

Yes. Order on this page and choose delivery, or WhatsApp the kitchen on 0821 9133 7592.

How much is a scooter a day?

From Rp 85K a day: Honda Beat 85K, Yamaha Gear 85K, Honda Scoopy 100K, Yamaha Fazzio 100K, Honda Vario 120K, Yamaha NMAX 150K, Honda PCX 150K, Honda ADV 175K, Yamaha XMAX 300K.

How do I book a scooter?

Choose a model and your dates on the scooter page and send the request. We confirm the bike and the price on WhatsApp, then you pick it up at the shop.

How do I pay?

Online payment is coming soon. For now we confirm your scooter booking on WhatsApp and you pay at the shop; food is paid at the counter.

How do TAKE Points work?

Sign in as a member with your email. You get 1 point for every Rp 10K you pay on food and scooter rentals, and 100 points take Rp 25K off your next order or rental.

Do you have drivers and tours?

Yes: Airport transfer, Sanur, Ubud, Nusa Dua, Uluwatu, Tanah Lot, Candi Dasa, Padang Bay, Trekking Mount Batur, Trekking Mount Agung, Full day tour. Send a request with the date and pick-up place.
