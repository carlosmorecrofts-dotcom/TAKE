From the kitchen

Made here, every day

TAKE is a small warung serving local and western food, Bali style, from breakfast to dinner. Mornings start with an Easy Breakfast of toast, potato wedges, chicken sausage and two eggs, a Simple Breakfast with avocado and grilled chicken, or a platter of papaya, pineapple, dragon fruit and watermelon.

Through the day the kitchen cooks Indonesian favourites like nasi goreng, mi goreng, fuyung hai and cap cay, next to pizzas from the Margherita to the Meat Lover, and spaghetti bolognese, carbonara or aglio olio.

Have a latte or cappuccino, hot or cold, a Bali coffee, a fresh juice, a young coconut or a cold Bintang at the wooden tables. Eat here, pick up or have it delivered, every day from 7am to 8pm.

Our riders

Picked up right here

Every photo here is a real TAKE rider, taken outside our shop in Perum Prapta Canggu Asri, a quiet lane off Jalan Pantai Berawa. Groups of friends and solo travellers all start the same way: pick a scooter at the shop and ride out into Canggu.

There is a scooter for every kind of trip. A Honda Beat, Scoopy or Yamaha Gear is easy to park for short hops to the beach and the cafés. A Honda Vario or Yamaha Fazzio suits everyday riding. For longer days out to Uluwatu, Tanah Lot or the hills, a Yamaha NMAX, Honda PCX or ADV gives you more comfort and power. Day prices start at Rp 85K.

Check the calendar for the days you need, book, and collect your scooter at the shop between 7am and 8pm. Bring your passport and driving licence, and WhatsApp us whenever you need a hand.

Questions

Good to know

Where is TAKE in Canggu?

In Perum Prapta Canggu Asri, Blok D No.1, on Jl. Krisnantara 1, a quiet lane off Jalan Pantai Berawa in Tibubeneng. TAKE Coffee and TAKE Scooter Rental share the white house with the two signs.

What are the opening hours?

Every day from 7am to 8pm, for the kitchen and the rental desk.

Do you deliver food?

Yes. Order on this page and choose delivery, or WhatsApp the kitchen on 0822 4016 0894.

How much is a scooter a day?

From Rp 85K a day: Honda Beat 85K, Yamaha Gear 85K, Honda Scoopy 100K, Yamaha Fazzio 100K, Honda Vario 120K, Yamaha NMAX 150K, Honda PCX 150K, Honda ADV 175K, Yamaha XMAX 300K.

How do I book a scooter?

Choose a model and your dates on the scooter page and send the request. We confirm the bike and the price on WhatsApp, then you pick it up at the shop.

How do I pay?

Online payment is coming soon. For now we confirm your scooter booking on WhatsApp and you pay at the shop; food is paid at the counter.

How do TAKE Points work?

Sign in as a member with your email. You get 1 point for every Rp 10K you pay on food and scooter rentals. Our first 100 members get Rp 200K off for every 100 points; after that, 100 points take Rp 25K off. Every 6 scooter rentals earn you a free day.

Do you have drivers and tours?

Yes: Airport transfer, Sanur, Ubud, Nusa Dua, Uluwatu, Tanah Lot, Candi Dasa, Padang Bay, Trekking Mount Batur, Trekking Mount Agung, Full day tour. Send a request with the date and pick-up place.
