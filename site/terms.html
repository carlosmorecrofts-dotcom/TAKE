You must be at least 18 years old [ADD: minimum age, if not 18], hold a valid driving licence for motorcycles, and be the person who rides the scooter. You book as a TAKE member with your own email.

2. Licence and documents

Before your first rental you upload, in your member area, a photo of your passport photo page and of your motorcycle driving licence. Visitors from abroad should carry an International Driving Permit that covers motorcycles, together with their home licence. You must carry your licence whenever you ride. At pick-up we check the documents against the person collecting the scooter. [ADD: do you keep the passport, a copy, or nothing during the rental?]
