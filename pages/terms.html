1. Who can rent

You must be at least 18 years old {{ADD_AGE}}, hold a valid driving licence for motorcycles, and be the person who rides the scooter. You book as a TAKE member with your own email.

2. Licence and documents

Before your first rental you upload, in your member area, a photo of your passport photo page and of your motorcycle driving licence. Visitors from abroad should carry an International Driving Permit that covers motorcycles, together with their home licence. You must carry your licence whenever you ride. At pick-up we check the documents against the person collecting the scooter. {{ADD_DOC_HOLD}}

3. Payment and deposit

You pay the full rental in advance when you book, online through our payment provider. The price is the day rate on our price list multiplied by the number of days. Deposit: {{ADD_DEPOSIT}}.

4. Pick-up and return

Pick up and return at the shop, Perum Prapta Canggu Asri Blok D No.1, between 7am and 8pm. Your rental runs from the pick-up date to the return date you chose when booking. At pick-up we note the odometer and the fuel level and check the scooter together with you. Return it clean of mud and in the condition you received it.

5. Fuel

Return the scooter with the same fuel level as at pick-up. {{ADD_FUEL}}

6. Damage, loss and theft

You are responsible for the scooter, its keys and helmets from pick-up until return. Damage: {{ADD_DAMAGE}}. Always lock the scooter and take the key. If it is stolen, report it to us and to the police straight away. Insurance: {{ADD_INSURANCE}}.

7. Accidents and police

After an accident, make sure everyone is safe, then contact us on WhatsApp at once and share your location from your booking page. Do not agree to pay anyone or leave the scooter with anyone without speaking to us. Traffic fines during your rental are yours to pay.

8. Late return and extensions

To keep the scooter longer, ask before your return date: we can only extend if it is not already booked. Late return without agreement: {{ADD_LATE}}.

9. Cancellations

{{ADD_CANCEL}}

10. Helmets, passengers and riding

Helmets are required by Indonesian law for rider and passenger. At most one passenger. No riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs, no racing, no stunts and no carrying loads the scooter is not built for.

12. Your documents and data

Your document photos are stored only for renting scooters to you. Only you and TAKE staff can open them. You can delete them in your member area at any time, after which we cannot rent you a scooter until you upload them again. Location is shared only while you press "Share my location" on your booking page, and that trail is deleted when the scooter is returned. {{ADD_RETENTION}}
